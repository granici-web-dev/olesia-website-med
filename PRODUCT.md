# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents of children aged 0–12 (and adults seeking nutrition guidance) in Moldova and the
diaspora. They reach the site worried, busy, and often far from a clinic — looking for
trustworthy pediatric and nutrition care without a three-week waitlist or a physical visit.
Their context: a phone or laptop, evenings, limited time, sometimes a sick child nearby.
The job to be done: "tell me which service fits my situation, prove I can trust this doctor,
and let me book or ask without friction."

## Product Purpose

The public marketing site for Dr. Olesea Jalba — a pediatrician with an MSc in Human
Nutrition — offering five online services in two formats: live video consultations (group A,
booked via Calendly) and portal-based support (group B: 3-month monitoring, 48h quick
question). The site exists to convert an anxious, uncertain visitor into a confident booking,
and to make the right service obvious. Success = a visitor lands, self-identifies their need,
trusts the doctor, and books or asks — bilingual (RO default, EN), no third-party CMS.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, expert. Speaks in second person ("tu"), plainly, without jargon or pressure —
"informație clară, fără presiune și fără mituri." A reassuring specialist, not a clinic
brochure and not a wellness influencer. Editorial restraint carried by serif display type and
a sage/cream palette; warmth comes from copy and rhythm, never from loud color.

## Anti-references

- Sterile hospital/clinic portals (cold blues, stock smiling-doctor photos, dense forms).
- Wellness-influencer aesthetics (gradients, emoji, hype, "transform your life").
- Generic SaaS pricing-page tropes (hero-metric template, identical card grids, popular-plan
  badges, urgency timers).
- Telehealth-startup cyan/green + Lexend monoculture.

## Design Principles

- **Make the right service obvious.** Five offerings is a choice-paralysis risk; the page must
  route by situation, not make the visitor decode formats.
- **Trust before transaction.** Credentials, clear scope, and honest disclaimers (not for
  emergencies) come before the CTA.
- **Calm over loud.** Hierarchy through type, space, and rhythm — not color or motion.
- **Describe here, price elsewhere.** The services page sells fit and clarity; pricing lives
  on /pricing.
- **Bilingual parity.** RO and EN copy carry equal craft; nothing reads machine-translated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 on its background; visible focus states; format/status never
conveyed by color alone (icon + text). Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Native semantics
(headings, landmarks, `<details>` FAQ, real table for comparison). Healthcare context: PII is
handled under GDPR; copy avoids alarmist or diagnostic language.
